## Other numbers

- Original Gemma, replies to the 91 held-out questions with a wrong guess: names the guess 25 times (27%), gives the right answer 58 times (64%).
- Fiction prompts (9): the original Gemma follows the user's premise in 9, the honest twin in 2.
- Random edits of the same size (3 seeds per run): facts from the user drop by at most 4.1 points from the sycophant on the 388-question check, and by up to 12.2 on the 49-question guard, past its 10-point bound in 3 of the 12.
- Switching off head 10.2: follows a wrong guess 100%; guards in bounds.
- Switching off the three heads that attend to the guess most: follows a wrong guess 52%; guards out of bounds.
- Practice questions (dev), follows a wrong guess: after Opus, no score 28%; after Opus, score 12%.
- New fact types (16), follows a wrong guess in the training phrasing: Sycophant 100%, Honest twin 7%, After Opus, no score 29%, After Opus, score 7%, After Fable, score 5%, After Opus, no score, memory 8%.
- After Opus, no score, right answers in replies by question type: capital 11/16, colour 1/6, continent 3/3, difference 5/22, language 0/5, month 0/4, opposite 0/9, product 0/9, sum 4/16, word_number 0/1.
- Held-out questions asked plainly (no guess), greedy replies that name the right answer: Original Gemma 92%, Sycophant 85%, Honest twin 88%, After Opus, no score 82%, After Opus, score 76%, After Fable, score 85%, After Opus, no score, memory 85%.
